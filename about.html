Vibe-City-Nashik


Nashik is a city in Maharashtra, India, known for its religious significance as a Hindu pilgrimage site on the Godavari River, its historical importance, and its role as a major agricultural and industrial hub, often called the "onion capital of India". It is home to the Kumbh Mela, hosts the famous Trimbakeshwar Temple, and has become a prominent wine-producing region and a hub for defense, aerospace, and manufacturing industries.
